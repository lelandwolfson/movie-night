Movie Night Shortlist

The 16 Drinker Recommends movies you haven't seen. Each of you marks In, Maybe or Pass, then filter down to the ones you both want.

"No passes" keeps any movie neither of you passed on. Votes are saved in this browser only, so mark both people's picks on the same device. Trailers need this page opened from a website; opened as a file on your computer, YouTube may refuse to play them.
